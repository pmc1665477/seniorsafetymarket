About SeniorSafetyMarket.com

The #1 marketplace to buy, sell, and donate used senior care and home safety equipment in the United States.

Who We Are

SeniorSafetyMarket.com is an online classified marketplace built exclusively for seniors, family caregivers, home health agencies, and assisted living facilities. We connect people across the United States who want to buy, sell, or donate used senior care equipment — without a middleman and without the clutter of general classifieds.

Whether you're a family clearing out a loved one's equipment, a caregiver upgrading a wheelchair, or a senior looking for an affordable lift chair, SeniorSafetyMarket.com gives you a safe, trusted place to connect.

Our Mission

Good used senior care equipment shouldn't end up in a landfill — and families shouldn't overpay for new gear when quality used equipment is available nearby. Our mission is to make it easy, free, and safe to buy, sell, and donate senior care equipment anywhere in the US.

What We List

SeniorSafetyMarket.com hosts listings for all major categories of senior care equipment including wheelchairs, walkers and rollators, lift chairs, electric hospital beds, power scooters, grab bars, shower chairs, stair lifts, ramps, bath lifts, oxygen equipment, and much more. Brands regularly listed include Drive Medical, Invacare, Pride Mobility, Golden Technologies, Medline, Carex, Sunrise Medical, and Stannah.

Our Story

SeniorSafetyMarket.com was founded by a St. Louis-based entrepreneur who recognized that families dealing with a loved one's care needs had nowhere dedicated to turn when buying or selling used equipment. General classifieds are full of irrelevant listings and feel unsafe. SeniorSafetyMarket.com was built to fill that gap — a clean, trustworthy, community-focused marketplace where families can help each other.
